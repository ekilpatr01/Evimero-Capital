About EVMC

Global experience,
local perspective

Founded in 2022, Evimero Capital (EVMC) is a private investment office drawing on extensive experience in corporate advisory, investment banking, private markets and family offices.

Our team brings global advisory and investment experience, having advised and invested alongside some of the world's most prominent corporations, families and investment firms.

We harness this global experience and perspective to guide our principal investment approach and to work with like-minded partners to identify and navigate strategic opportunities and investments.

What We Do

Our capabilities

01

Principal Investment

EVMC invests directly and partners with investment managers globally across a broadly diversified portfolio of public equities, credit, real estate, private equity and other alternatives.

02

Investment Advisory

EVMC actively partners with like-minded institutional and private clients to provide investment and transactional support on investment opportunities, principally in private market transactions.

03

M&A, Capital Raising & Strategic Advisory

EVMC acts as a trusted advisor to companies, boards and executives, providing high quality, independent advice across a full spectrum of M&A, capital raising and broader corporate strategic matters.

Our Approach

Institutional pedigree,
privileged access

Drawing on decades of transactional and investment experience across global financial centres, EVMC brings institutional rigour to every situation — whether investing or advising alongside family offices, corporates, or global institutions.
